## Competitor and category patterns
These are general observations about how established Indian jewellery brands present themselves online (national chains, Bengaluru heritage jewellers). They are design inputs, not claims about Svarahi.
- **Product-first heroes:** macro gold on dark or warm grounds, rather than logos.
- **Discovery:** shop-by-occasion (bridal, festive, daily wear) and shop-by-craft (temple, antique, kundan, polki).
- **Store conversion:** "book a store visit" or "try at home" CTAs, store locators, and WhatsApp concierge.
- **Trust strips:** hallmarking, certified diamonds, exchange policy. Every one of these needs owner confirmation for Svarahi.
- **Bridal:** treated as an editorial world of its own, with campaigns and lookbooks.

**Svarahi's angle:**
- A single Jayanagar showroom.
- Manufacturer-direct.
- Temple and antique gold strength, judging by its own Instagram.
- A personal, in-store buying experience.

So the site should create desire online and convert to a visit or a WhatsApp conversation.
