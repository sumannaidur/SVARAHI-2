01 · Homepage

A cinematic first impression

  • Jewellery fills the screen, not the logo.
  • Scroll-driven scenes walk through discovering, understanding, experiencing and owning a piece.
  • WhatsApp, Visit and Directions are always one tap away on phones.

The hero image is an AI-generated editorial concept, labelled as such on the page. It is a placeholder until Svarahi's own photography is supplied.

02 · Collections & discovery

A showroom, laid out

  • Eight collections, filters by collection and style, and a code for every piece (SV-N01…).
  • Every card has "Enquire" (a prefilled WhatsApp message) and "+ Trial tray".

Where Svarahi has no photograph of a piece yet, the page says "Photograph to follow" rather than showing someone else's jewellery.

03 · Piece page

Look closer, then ask

  • The image zooms in (by mouse, tap or keyboard).
  • The page shows the catalogue code, collection and style, plus WhatsApp, call and email enquiry and the trial tray.
  • A "You may also like" rail and next/previous browsing.

Images are labelled "Style reference · not the actual piece". No price, weight or purity is shown until Svarahi supplies verified details.

04 · The virtual counter

Lay it out on velvet before you visit

  • Choose a collection and the pieces appear on a velvet counter.
  • Drag a piece onto the trial tray, or tap to add it (up to eight).
  • Send the tray on WhatsApp or by email, or plan a visit with it.

The tray is saved only on the visitor's device. Nothing is sent until they choose to, and there are no accounts and no checkout.

05 · Craftsmanship

A jeweller's loupe

  • A magnifying lens over a macro photograph, showing real detail from the full-resolution image.
  • Five crafts unfold as you scroll: nakshi, granulation, stone setting, kundan and antique finish.
  • The page ends with Svarahi's own care guidance.

The crafts are described in general terms only. Nothing claims an in-house workshop until Svarahi confirms it.

06 · Instagram reels

Real Svarahi jewellery, in motion

  • Svarahi's own reels and posts, through Instagram's official embed.
  • One item loads at a time, and nothing plays until the visitor presses play.

Posts that show customers or staff are excluded until consent is given. One reel (DfwJDxcD1Vg) cannot currently be embedded: Instagram reports it as unavailable.

07 · Bridal & heritage

Chosen together, worn for a lifetime

  • A curved carousel of nine bridal and heritage looks.
  • Each look has "Ask about a bridal set in this style".
  • The page explains how a bridal trial works and offers pieces for the trial tray.

The looks are licensed editorial photographs or AI concepts, each captioned and never shown as Svarahi inventory.

08 · Visit & concierge

From browsing to booked

  • The visitor chooses an occasion, a day, a time of day and their trial tray.
  • They see the exact message before it opens in WhatsApp or email.
  • The page also explains what to expect at the counter, lists the concierge contacts and gives directions.

Opening hours read "to be confirmed" until Svarahi approves them, and the site stores nothing.

09 · Svarahi Gold

Connected, but separate

  • A bridge page explains the difference between jewellery here and bullion there.
  • It opens svarahigold.xyz in a new tab.

No rates, accounts or trading on this site, and nothing is shared between the two.

Built on honesty

What the site will and won't say

AreaRule on the site today
PhotographyEvery image is classified. Licensed editorial (C), showroom inspiration (D) and AI concepts (F) are captioned and never presented as Svarahi pieces, customers or the showroom. 36 images of unknown origin from svarahi.co are blocked from public pages.
Commercial claimsOnly "Jewellery straight from manufacturers" is approved. The other 15 claims (V.A. 2.5 %, 0 % making, ₹123/g, Gold+ 7.5 %, buyback, hallmark/HUID, hours, rating…) stay hidden until approved.
Prices & purchaseNo prices, weights, purity or live rates. Purchases are made in the showroom: weight × the day's rate, with making charge and GST shown separately.
InstagramOnly Svarahi's own posts without people, through the official embed. No media is copied.
PrivacyNo accounts, forms that post, or tracking. The trial tray and performance metrics stay on the visitor's device.
Search enginesThe whole site is blocked from indexing (noindex and robots.txt) until you approve launch. One switch turns it on.
