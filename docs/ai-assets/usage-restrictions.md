# Svarahi Flagship V2 — Asset Classification & Usage Restrictions Policy

## 1. Classification Definition: GROUP F

All visual assets produced via Nano Banana 2 in this directory (`public/assets/generated/nano-banana/`) are officially designated as:

> **GROUP F — AI-GENERATED EDITORIAL CONCEPT**

These assets are creative representations and conceptual mood imagery designed solely to support luxury atmosphere, editorial storytelling, website design aesthetic, and interactive staging.

---

## 2. Authenticity Boundaries (Strict Prohibitions)

Group F assets must adhere to strict truth-in-advertising and brand integrity boundaries:

1. **Not Real Svarahi Inventory**:
   - The jewellery designs, stones, carvings, and settings depicted in Group F imagery do not represent manufactured Svarahi SKUs.
   - They must **NEVER** be used as primary product imagery on catalogue cards, inventory specification sheets, pricing modules, or e-commerce checkout flows.
2. **Not Real Svarahi Customers**:
   - The models depicted are entirely fictional, AI-synthesized characters.
   - They must never be portrayed as actual customers, brides, brand ambassadors, or real individuals.
   - No fake customer reviews, testimonials, or social handles may be attached to these portraits.
3. **Not the Actual Svarahi Showroom**:
   - The architectural visualizations and showroom renderings represent an aspirational contemporary concept.
   - They must **NEVER** be presented as actual photographs of Svarahi’s physical store in Jayanagar, Bengaluru.
   - When displayed on the showroom/experience page, they must be explicitly accompanied by a disclaimer: *"Conceptual Showroom Visualisation"*.
4. **No Artificial Hallmarks or Logos**:
   - Generative tools must never fabricate BIS 916 hallmarks, HUID codes, gemstone certification seals, or official logos.
   - Authentic branding and certifications must be rendered via clean SVG and validated metadata only.
5. **No Fake Manufacturing Claims**:
   - Macro craftsmanship imagery (`ai-craftsmanship-macro-001`) serves as an aesthetic concept for traditional Indian goldsmithing techniques.
   - It must not be captioned as Svarahi’s proprietary in-house workshop without verification.

---

## 3. Permitted Usage Scenarios

Group F assets are explicitly approved for the following digital design touchpoints:

| Category | Permitted Use Case | Example |
|---|---|---|
| **Editorial Hero** | Full-bleed hero backgrounds and mood banners behind typographic headlines | `hero-bridal-campaign.webp` |
| **Category Moodboard** | Visual backdrop cards for category exploration (e.g., "The Bridal Pavilion", "Festive Splendour") | `lookbook-bridal-01.webp` |
| **Styling Guides** | Editorial inspiration guides illustrating how traditional gold is worn across occasions | `lookbook-mens-01.webp`, `lookbook-everyday-01.webp` |
| **Virtual Counter Stage** | Reusable empty stage backdrop where genuine Svarahi cutouts are layered dynamically | `velvet-counter-stage-01.webp` |
| **Texture Accents** | Full-width background panels, card textures, navigation drawers, and ambient dividers | `texture-oxblood-velvet-01.webp`, `texture-brushed-gold-01.webp` |

---

## 4. Front-End Deployment Protocol

- **Approval Gate**: No Group F asset may be merged into production customer-facing page templates without prior visual sign-off via the Visual Contact Sheet (`docs/ai-assets/visual-contact-sheet.html`).
- **Separation of Concerns**: Real inventory product cards must exclusively pull from Group A (Verified Original Product Photographs) once provided.
